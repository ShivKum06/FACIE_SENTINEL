SQL injection pattern
Invoke-WebRequest "https://facie-sentinel.onrender.com/search?q=union%20select%20password%20from%20users"
Expected: 403, INJECTION, BLOCK.

JSON body injection
Invoke-WebRequest `
  -Uri "https://facie-sentinel.onrender.com/api/v1/user/profile" `
  -Method POST `
  -ContentType "application/json" `
  -Body '{"display_name":"Test","bio":"union select password from users"}'
After sending them, refresh the dashboard. You should see INJECTION incidents with high risk and BLOCK actions.
